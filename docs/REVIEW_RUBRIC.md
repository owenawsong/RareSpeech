# Listening rubric

Reviewers must understand the language and the term, or establish accepted pronunciations from reliable references. Model identity is visible in the diagnostic report; this is not a blinded listening study.

- **Pass:** this occurrence is present in sequence and pronounced acceptably for the declared language/accent.
- **Fail:** omitted, substituted, or unmistakably mispronounced under a documented acceptance rule.
- **Uncertain:** unfamiliar term, ambiguous localization, undocumented legitimate variant, or unclear audio.

Listen to the relevant context. Do not decide from ASR spelling alone. Review apparent successes as well as failures. The collection currently lacks expert pronunciation validation; establish accepted variants before claiming a verified ranking.

The UI enables decisions after playback as a workflow nudge, not proof of careful listening. Decisions stay in local storage until exported. Use pseudonyms, not emails; exported pseudonyms are public metadata if the artifact is published.

Two distinct agreeing pseudonyms are the minimum consensus policy. They must represent genuinely independent qualified people to support that claim. Uncertain/conflicting votes withhold the verified headline. Adjudication requires an explicit, auditable replacement of previous decisions.
